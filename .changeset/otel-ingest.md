The otel Module: Signals stored in the OpenTelemetry data model (`otel_logs`, `otel_metrics_sum`, `otel_metrics_gauge`) and OTLP/HTTP JSON ingest at `/v1/logs`, `/v1/metrics` and `/v1/traces`, with gzip, `partialSuccess` refusals, 413 and 415, idempotent retries, promoted `embertrail.*` Session-link columns, retention on `time`, and an OTLP JSON Lines export.
